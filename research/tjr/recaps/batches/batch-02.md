## 2. Recurring patterns
- **He sits out on news-heavy days** (Powell testimony, NFP, CPI) or waits for the release first.
  This is the opposite of Feb 22 in batch 1, when he traded through news.
- **Every plan is conditional:**
  - a pullback into a marked level or zone, or a sweep of a marked high/low;
  - then a structure break on the 5m or 15m;
  - then entry.
  The levels are prior session highs/lows and 1h/4h swing points.
- **The timeframe stack is unchanged:** the 4h sets the bias, the 15m or 5m gives the trigger.

## 3. Contradictions and missing information
- **The news rule is inconsistent.** He traded through news on Feb 22 but sat out Mar 6–8 and 12.
  There's no stated objective rule: which events, and how long before or after.
- **The plans have no stops or targets** except 011's ES idea (target ≈5166).
- **Nothing can be scored.** The videos end right after the open.

## 4. Hypotheses (updated)
- **H7 (red-news days are worse)** gets a behavioural data point: he mostly skips them. To make it
  testable, define "news day" = a red-impact US release (CPI, NFP, FOMC, Powell testimony, PPI)
  scheduled that session.
- **New, H8:** the entry trigger needs **two steps**. First, price reaches a pre-marked level:
  a sweep of a prior high/low, or a pullback into a zone. Second, a lower-timeframe close confirms
  the direction (BOS or IFVG). A level touched without that close = no trade.
