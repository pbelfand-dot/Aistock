# TJR rulebook (to be filled from the videos)

Every rule must be exact enough to code. Anything the videos leave vague is listed under
"Open questions" with the choice we made and why.

Starting point, from public descriptions of his model (to be confirmed or corrected by the
videos): price **sweeps liquidity** (takes out a prior high/low where stops sit), the market
**shifts structure** the other way on a lower timeframe, then price **returns to the fair value
gap** left by that move, which is the entry, targeting the opposite liquidity.
