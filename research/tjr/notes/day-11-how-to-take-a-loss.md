# Boot Camp Day 11: How to take a loss

Source: video (≈18 min, talking to camera). Rough offline transcript.

## What he teaches
- Trading is a game of percentages: nobody wins 100% of trades, and losses are part of it.
  Judge results over weeks and months, not single trades.
- After a loss, ask "what can I learn?", not "why me?". Getting angry means you did something
  wrong (usually over-risked or took one trade too many).
- Never revenge-trade or over-leverage after a loss. His own worst days came from that.
- **Practice on demo (paper) first**, lose there, learn there, then go live and change nothing.
- Follow the plan "robotically", step by step.
- **Take days off** when you can't trade well: big news days, or when you're emotionally off.

## For the bot
- Evaluate the strategy on many trades (the 30-trading-day paper stages), never on one.
- No doubling-up or bigger trades after losses: size only from the fixed risk rules.
- **News days:** a rule to skip entries on major scheduled US releases (Fed decisions, CPI, jobs
  report) until we have evidence it helps or hurts. To confirm with his "news" lesson if one comes.
