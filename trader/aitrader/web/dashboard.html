    Pause trading?

    Both desks stop making new trades. Nothing is sold now. The bot still protects what it owns: stop-losses stay on, and the day desk still sells before the close (it never holds overnight). Resume from Setup when you're ready.

    Emergency stop

    Cancels the bot's orders and sells everything the bot owns right away, then halts both desks. Your own investments are never touched.

    Start Stage 1?

    The swing desk starts paper trading now, with pretend money, and skips the study month. It owns the strongest stocks from your watchlist and the daily all-stocks scan (their 12-month rise), buys only while a stock and the S&P 500 are above their 200-day averages, and sells when a stock weakens. Stop-losses, loss limits and good faith violation rules all apply.

    Honest expectation, from 16 years of data: about the market's return plus a little, with bigger drops. Thirty trading days can catch a broken method but can't prove a good one.

    Start Stage 2?

    The day desk starts paper trading TJR's model with pretend money: while a stock's hourly trend is up, it waits for price to sweep below a low where stops sit, break back above the last swing high, and pull back into the gap that move left. Buys 9:35-11:30am, stop just below the sweep, target twice the risk, one trade per stock a day, everything sold before the close.

    Stage 1 keeps running on the swing desk. Loss limits, the kill switch and good faith rules all apply.

      What happens next

      Autopilot

      The bot runs in the background on this Mac. Keep it on and the Mac plugged in.

      Autopilot

      It starts when you log in and restarts itself if it stops.

      Keep trading with the lid closed

      While it's plugged in, the Mac stays awake with the lid closed. On battery it sleeps as usual, so it never runs hot in a bag. It asks for your Mac password once. While this is on and the Mac is plugged in, Apple menu → Sleep does nothing; turn this off first.

      Brokers & keys

      Every key is saved only on this Mac (in ~/AITrader/.env), never on GitHub. Saving real-money keys turns nothing on: real money always needs a typed confirmation in the full Setup menu.

      Alpaca paper account

      Free, with pretend money. This is where Stage 1 paper trades.

      How do I get these keys?

      Sign up at Alpaca, switch to Paper Trading, open API Keys → Generate, and paste both keys here. Without keys, the bot simulates paper trading on this Mac.

        Alpaca real-money keys

        Only needed once a desk earns real money.

        Webull

        Paper trading at Webull: Settings → Paper trading happens at: Webull. Real money at Webull needs its real-money keys, Settings → Broker for real money: Webull, and the usual typed OK.

        How do I get Webull keys?

        1. On the Webull website: API Management → My Application → apply (Webull reviews it in about 1–2 business days). 2. Once approved: Generate Key (Webull texts you a code) and paste the App Key and App Secret here. 3. Press Test Webull, then approve Kestrel in the Webull app within 5 minutes. Pick Paper for Webull test-environment keys (if you pick wrong, Test Webull switches it).

          Charles Schwab

          From developer.schwab.com → your app: the App Key, the Secret, and the callback address exactly as you registered it.

          Log in to Schwab (about once a week)

          Schwab's login lasts 7 days. 1. Open Schwab login, sign in and approve. 2. Your browser ends on a page that won't load; that's expected. Copy that page's whole address and paste it here.

            Your phone

            Alerts for every trade and anything urgent, and the Kestrel screen on your iPhone. Use any or all.

            Pushover push alerts

            Every buy and sell, the after-market summary, and anything urgent: loud enough to wake you for a real-money emergency.

            How do I set up Pushover?

            1. Install Pushover on your iPhone (free for 30 days, then $4.99 once) and sign up at pushover.net. 2. Copy Your User Key from your Pushover dashboard. 3. Create an application named Kestrel and copy its API Token. 4. Paste both here and press Save: a test alert comes right away.

            Telegram: alerts and commands

            A message for every trade and the after-market report, and control from your phone: /status, /trades, /report, /pause, /resume, /kill SELL EVERYTHING, /help. Only your phone is ever answered.

            How do I set up Telegram?

            1. In Telegram, open @BotFather, send /newbot and pick any name; it gives you a token. 2. Paste the token here and press Save. 3. Open your new bot and send the pairing message shown below.

            Alert me if Kestrel stops

            A Mac that's asleep, unplugged or offline can't warn you itself. Kestrel checks in with healthchecks.io (free) every 5 minutes; if the check-ins stop, or trading keeps failing for 15 minutes, healthchecks.io alerts your phone.

            How do I set it up?

            1. Sign up free at healthchecks.io. 2. Add a check, set its schedule to Cron */5 9-15 * * 1-5, time zone America/New_York, grace time 15 minutes (it then only expects Kestrel while the market is open). 3. Under Integrations, add Pushover or Telegram (or keep email). 4. Copy the check's Ping URL (https://hc-ping.com/…), paste it here and press Save: Kestrel pings it right away.

            Kestrel screen on your iPhone

            This screen on your iPhone from anywhere, with Pause and Emergency stop. It goes over Tailscale (free), a private network between your own devices.

            How do I set it up?

            1. Install Tailscale on this Mac and your iPhone and log in to the same account on both. 2. Turn this on. 3. Open the link on your phone, then Share → Add to Home Screen. Keys and settings stay on the Mac.

            AI & news

            The local AI that writes the plain-English parts, and the news Kestrel reads. None of these decide trades.

            Local AI (Gemma)

            Runs on this Mac and writes the plan, the after-market summary and the team's notes. Nothing is sent anywhere. Needs Ollama (free), opened once; Kestrel downloads the model by itself.

            Economic news and SEC filings

            FRED: the dates of CPI, the jobs report and Fed decisions, with a countdown. SEC: official company filings for what it owns or considers; serious ones stop it buying.

            Where do I get these?

            A free FRED key: fredaccount.stlouisfed.org (sign up, then API Keys → Request API Key). The SEC needs no key, but asks every program for a contact email, sent with each request and nowhere else.
