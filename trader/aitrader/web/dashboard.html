    Pause trading?

    Both desks stop making new trades. Nothing is sold now. The bot still protects what it owns: stop-losses stay on, and the day desk still sells before the close (it never holds overnight). Resume from the AI Trader menu when you're ready.

    Emergency stop

    Cancels the bot's orders and sells everything the bot owns right away, then halts both desks. Your own investments are never touched.
