    Pause trading?

    Both desks stop making new trades. Nothing is sold now. The bot still protects what it owns: stop-losses stay on, and the day desk still sells before the close (it never holds overnight). Resume from Setup when you're ready.

    Emergency stop

    Cancels the bot's orders and sells everything the bot owns right away, then halts both desks. Your own investments are never touched.

    Start Stage 1?

    The swing desk starts paper trading now, with pretend money, and skips the study month. It owns the strongest stocks from your watchlist and the daily all-stocks scan (their 12-month rise), buys only while a stock and the S&P 500 are above their 200-day averages, and sells when a stock weakens. Stop-losses, loss limits and good faith violation rules all apply.

    Honest expectation, from 16 years of data: about the market's return plus a little, with bigger drops. Thirty trading days can catch a broken method but can't prove a good one.

    Setup

    1 Connect your Alpaca paper account

    Free, with pretend money. Sign up at Alpaca, switch to Paper Trading, open API Keys → Generate, and paste both keys here. They're saved only on this Mac. (Optional: without keys the bot simulates paper trading on this Mac.)

      2 Autopilot

      Runs the bot in the background: it starts when you log in and restarts itself if it stops. Keep the Mac plugged in; closing the lid puts it to sleep.

      Get a message for every trade, the after-close results and the after-market report, and control Kestrel from your phone (/status, /trades, /report, /pause). 1.       6 Kestrel screen on your phone (Tailscale)

      See this screen on your iPhone from anywhere, with the Pause and Emergency stop buttons. It goes over Tailscale (free): a private network between your own devices, never the open internet. 1. Install Tailscale on this Mac and your iPhone and log in to the same account on both. 2. Turn this on. 3. Open the link on your phone, then Share → Add to Home Screen. Keys and settings stay on the Mac.
